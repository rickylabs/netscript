Context. B1, first saga leaf, on the 0.0.9 critical path. Read the whole issue. The saga engine reserves a message's idempotency (applied) key before the handler runs and saves the transition afterwards, with no release on failure, so after a crash or store error every same-key redelivery is a silent no-op. On KV (the scaffold default) the retry of a failed handler is skipped. Scheduled and cascaded effects are dispatched after the transition is saved, so a crash in that window loses them.
Deliver, in this order (one slice each, commit and push after each):
1. The applied key, the new envelope and the transition record are committed in one crash-safe atomic store operation after the handler succeeds, through an explicit store capability on the saga store port. Releasing a reservation on failure is not an acceptable substitute: a process killed after the reservation runs no cleanup. KV (the scaffold default) first, then every other saga store adapter through the same port.
2. The effects outbox: the transition's scheduled and cascaded effects are written in the same atomic operation, and a dispatcher drains the outbox with stable keys after a restart (SagaOutboxPort is the reserved seam). Coordinate the schema with the existing worker-command persistence from #1932.
