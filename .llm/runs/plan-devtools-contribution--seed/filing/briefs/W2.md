## Do not

- Do not write a second divergent generator. Emission is a generator concern and belongs beside the
  existing plugin-registry generators; a parallel implementation recreates the two-generator defect.
- Do not hand-roll AST extraction with regexes.
- Do not perform per-target non-transactional writes, and do not leave a registry entry behind on
  plugin removal.
- Do not deepen `@netscript/cli`'s existing **Restructure** verdict — no new cross-layer reach.
- Do not resolve anything by plugin **name** on the host side; resolve by `mountId`. Host-side
  plugin-name coupling is one of the two classes `quality:scan` exists to catch.
- Do not run the full `e2e:cli run scaffold.runtime` on every intermediate loop; it is a
  merge-readiness gate, not a per-slice gate.
