## Hard dependencies

- **W1-a is the wave root.** W1-b and W1-c depend on it; W2-a depends on W1-a + W1-c.
- **L13 is not optional.** Adding `--root packages/devtools-core` and `--root plugins/devtools` to
  `deno.json`'s `arch:check` ships **inside W1-a**. `arch:check` gates 16 of 36 live units today; a
  new package inherits no mechanical doctrine gate without that line, and every gate claim in RFC
  §13.3 is decorative until it lands.
- **W1-d is gated on manifest reality, not on our preference.** The `#890` claim that "older CLIs
  ignore an unknown block" is false — the schema is `.strict()`. Prove the current behavior with a
  test before choosing the contract.

## Owner forks that must be resolved first

- **F-1 (#890 spine vs self-contained family) — blocks W1-a.** It decides the public package home
  and import specifiers, emitter ownership, the dependency graph, and whether #922 needs
  re-baselining. Do not start W1-a on a recommendation; require the ratified answer.
- **F-3 (manifest schema evolution) — blocks W1-d**, and W1-d in turn blocks any manifest-visible
  pointer. `.passthrough()` and `schemaVersion: 2` have different old-CLI behavior *and different
  tests*.
- F-8 (archetype) is **already resolved** in RFC §13.1: A1 contracts + A6 CLI + A5 plugin, with the
  A3 trigger written down. Do not re-litigate it; if supervised state later moves into a published
  package, that package is A3 and gate F-13 applies from its first slice.

## Do not

- Do not add ports, adapters, DI, base classes, or any IO to `packages/devtools-core` — A1 forbids
  them, and inventing a port for a single adapter is the Wet Codebase failure doctrine names.
- Do not let `devtools-core` depend on `@netscript/fresh` or `@netscript/fresh-ui`.
- Do not introduce a `DevToolsContribution` union (AP-3) or a `switch (contribution.kind)` (AP-24).
- Do not widen the closed unions to `string`, and do not omit an explicit return type — both fail
  `deno doc --lint` slow-types, which is the publish bar.
- Do not add a `// deno-lint-ignore` or `as unknown as` to green a wrapper. That is a
  review-blocking finding, not a pass.
- Do not exceed two subpaths at v1: `.` and `./contracts/v1`. No barrel re-export of upstream.
- Do not touch #922's children or `plugins/*` in this wave.
