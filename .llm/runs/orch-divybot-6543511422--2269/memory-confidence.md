# Guidance over the complete corpus

Absolute BM25 score is insufficient confidence after expanding the corpus. Confidence checks
IDF-weighted coverage of original caller terms (minimum 0.6), treats recognized aliases as bridges
only for a supported concept, and caps unknown uncovered qualifiers at low. Negative controls
include quantum entanglement without polling: a cache concept must not erase unknown qualifiers.

Page context gives other scored sections of the strongest page at most half its score, capped
below the anchor. This preserves related sections in the ownership-vs-integration golden fixture
without replacing its expected routes with unrelated auth or Aspire pages.
