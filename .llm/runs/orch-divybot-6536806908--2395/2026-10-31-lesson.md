# Lesson — 2026-10-31

A lease design must lock the ownership fence and recovery grace before implementation.
Watching the shared processing list makes unrelated consumers conflict; watching the ready
list adds producer interference. Retaining blocking pickup requires an explicit recovery
protocol for the pickup-to-stamp crash gap. That recovery must not adopt a healthy worker's
new entry immediately: observe it across a grace interval and fence stamp/adoption races.

A failed plan gate is evidence, not permission to implement. Preserve both evaluator
verdicts and leave a concrete coordinator decision when permitted rounds are exhausted.


The authorized second cycle passed after using atomic server-side scripts and including the
Garnet Lua launch prerequisites in both managed launch functions. XAUTOCLAIM is unavailable on
the pinned Garnet. Independent review then found idle and busy-handler outage regressions:
test both paths, not just worker crashes. Listener operations must preserve baseline availability,
while receipt fencing makes late settlement safe. External DLQ delivery must run independently
of consumption and recovery. Real process/proxy regressions and old-adapter negative controls
are the meaningful proof; compilation alone misses these failure boundaries.
