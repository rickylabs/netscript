# Lesson — 2026-10-31

A lease design must lock the ownership fence and recovery grace before implementation.
Watching the shared processing list makes unrelated consumers conflict; watching the ready
list adds producer interference. Retaining blocking pickup requires an explicit recovery
protocol for the pickup-to-stamp crash gap. That recovery must not adopt a healthy worker's
new entry immediately: observe it across a grace interval and fence stamp/adoption races.

A failed plan gate is evidence, not permission to implement. Preserve both evaluator
verdicts and leave a concrete coordinator decision when permitted rounds are exhausted.
